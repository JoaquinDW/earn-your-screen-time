# Earnit — Meta static-ad starter set

Three 4:5 (1080 × 1350) PNGs designed for Facebook and Instagram feed placements. Each asset uses only documented product mechanics and privacy facts; no outcome metrics, testimonials, or fabricated social proof.

## 01 — Three ways to earn

**Asset:** `01-move-first-scroll-later.png`  
**Template:** Grid static — A-tier, cold-capable  
**Angle:** Choice and constructive friction.  
**Grounded in:** Product Marketing Context v1: users can earn time via Apple Health steps, focused study, or on-device push-up counting; access is spent in deliberate, bounded sessions.

| Meta field | Copy | Characters |

## Launch notes

- Use a 1% or Advantage+ prospecting audience for `01`; it is the broadest product-mechanism introduction.
- Use `02` and `03` as complementary variants or in retargeting: they answer the "too restrictive" and privacy objections respectively.
- Replace or extend this starter set once account results, real reviews, and ad comments exist. Do not add invented customer quotes or outcomes.
