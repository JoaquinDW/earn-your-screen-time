# Background photo slots

The posts already reference these exact filenames in `images/`. Add your own
generated or original photos, then render again. No photos are generated or
downloaded by this tool. Missing photos use each slide's solid fallback.
Use 1080 × 1920 or larger, 9:16, JPEG. To use PNG/WebP, update `slide.image`.

Shared direction: a candid, slightly imperfect phone photo, warm and dim,
cozy everyday surroundings, believable grain and uneven exposure. No studio
lighting, polished stock-photo look, typography, watermarks, visible logos,
readable text or actual app UI. Any phone is generic with a dark screen.
Leave quiet negative space in the middle for added captions. Keep key objects
clear of the top 150 px, bottom 400 px and right 120 px.

- **`bed-night.jpg` — f1 / f2 / f3 / f5:** Vertical candid photo from the edge
  of an unmade bed late at night. A hand loosely holds a generic phone near a
  rumpled duvet; screen dark and angled away. One warm bedside lamp, soft
  shadows, ordinary small bedroom, a slightly awkward real-phone crop. No
  face needed. Keep the center softly lit and uncluttered for white captions.
- **`walk-dusk.jpg` — f3 / f4 / f5:** Vertical candid photo of a person walking
  away along a quiet neighborhood sidewalk at dusk, from a friend's phone
  several steps behind. Ordinary clothes and sneakers, warm windows, slight
  motion blur, uneven pavement, muted evening sky. No visible phone UI or
  signage. Leave the central upper-middle area simple for caption text.
- **`living-room-pushups.jpg` — f1 / f2 / f4:** Vertical low-angle candid phone
  photo of an adult doing a push-up on an ordinary living-room floor, fully
  clothed in casual exercise clothes. Sofa at the edge, warm standing lamp,
  modest clutter, imperfect framing and natural grain. No fitness-studio
  aesthetic. No camera overlays, counters, text or screens. Keep the center
  dark enough for white text or a phone mockup.
- **`phone-facedown.jpg` — f1 / f2 / f3 / f4 / f5:** Vertical candid phone photo
  of a generic smartphone face-down on a wooden table in the evening, beside
  a half-finished cup of tea. Warm side lighting, a few everyday marks on the
  table, no luxury styling. No visible logos or screen. Wide quiet middle
  area, natural deep shadows, slightly imperfect exposure.
- **`desk-night.jpg` — f1 / f2 / f3 / f4 / f5:** Vertical candid phone photo of
  an ordinary desk at night, warm string lights in the distance, a mug,
  closed laptop and a pen at the margins. The central desk area is clear.
  Cozy low light, believable grain and a slightly tilted camera angle. No
  readable writing, book covers, logos or visible device UI.
- **`candle-journal.jpg` — f1 / f4 / f5:** Vertical candid phone photo of a
  small candle and a closed, unmarked journal on a bedside table. Rumpled
  blanket at one edge, warm dim light, soft focus in the distance, lived-in
  rather than styled. No visible words or writing, no logos. Leave generous
  quiet center space for captions.

These are backgrounds only. Earnit's phone screens come from the repository's
existing screenshot HTML; do not generate product interfaces into the photos.
