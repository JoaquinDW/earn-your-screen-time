# Reference slideshows (found on TikTok, 2026-10-29)

These are screenshots of real posts, kept for reference only. Copy the structure and the visual style, never the images or the text.

## Example A: "things that helped me reduce/stop doom scrolling" (`ex-a-*.png`)

Found by searching "opal app". It has 6 slides; Opal is probably one of the later tips.

- **Look:** native TikTok, not a designed ad. Each slide is a real, dim, cozy phone photo: a desk at night with fairy lights, a candle and an open journal. There's no brand color and no layout grid.
- **Text:** TikTok's own in-app text styles, set in lowercase and written casually ("create a morning/night routine!!").
  - The hook is white text with a soft shadow in the center of the photo.
  - Tip slides have a white title with a black outline, and the body sits below it in yellow text with a blue outline. That's TikTok's "outlined" text style.
- **Structure:** slide 1 is the hook over a photo. Each later slide gives one tip, with a short title and 2–4 lines explaining why. The app shows up as one tip among several, not as an ad.
- **For Earnit:** this matches format F1 (listicle). Its main style should be this native "photo + TikTok text" look, not the App Store ivory/navy design. The Earnit tip slide can show a real app screen held in a hand or lying on the desk, or a plain screenshot.

## Example B: "Apps to use instead of doomscrolling:" (`ex-b-*.png`)

- **Look:** clean, light-gray background, bold black sans-serif headings (Inter-like), real app icons.
- **Slide 1 (hook):** the headline, then the "bad" app icons on the left (Threads, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X), a hand-drawn arrow, and the "good" app icons on the right (TED, NYT Games, Duolingo, Deepstash, etc.).
- **Slides 2+:** one app per slide. Its icon and name sit at the top left, with a one-line description beside them, and below are 3 phone mockups with real screenshots.
- **For Earnit:** this is a new format, **F6 app list**. Earnit appears as one of the "good" apps, and its slide uses three real screens (shield, push-up counter, sessions). The other apps should be genuine, non-competing alternatives (Duolingo, a reading app, NYT Games, a running app). Never include screen-time blockers like Opal or One Sec.
